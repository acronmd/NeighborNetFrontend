## Introduction 

What is NeighborNet? 
NeighborNet is a community-based social networking app that helps you connect neighbors, share local stories, 
organize events, and build a stronger community. Whether you want to: 
- Share news and updates with your neighborhood
- Organize or join local events
- Chat with neighbors
- View community activities on a map
- Earn badges for community participation

NeighborNet makes it easy to stay connected with the people around you.

System Requirements
- Android: Android device running Android 8.0 or later
- Active internet connection (WiFi or mobile data)

## Getting Started

For Android:
1. Open the Google Play Store on your Android device
2. Tap the Search bar at the top
3. Type "NeighborNet"
4. Tap Install next to the NeighborNet app
5. Accept the permissions when prompted
6. Wait for the installation to complete
7. Tap OPEN or find the NeighborNet icon in your app drawer

## Main Features

Posts show you: 
- Author's name and profile picture
- Post content (text and/or image)
- Time posted
- Number of likes and comments

- Authors's name 
- Number of people allowed to attend
- Location and time of the event

### Messaging & Chat: Connect directly with your neighbors through private messages and group chats.

Trusted Contacts
- In order to be able to message someone you need to be a trusted contact or else it will not allow you to 

Direct Messages (DM)
1. Tap the Chat icn at the navigation bar
2. Tap New button at the top right
3. Select a contact from your list
4. Type your message in the text box at the bottom
5. Tap Send

Types of messages you can send
- Audio recordings 
- files 
- text messages 
- photos 

Reading Messages:
- Tap on any conversation to open it
- Read receipts show ✓✓ when the other person has seen your message
- Online status shows "Active now" when the person is currently using the app

Message Options:
- Edit Message:
    1. Long-press on your sent message
    2. Tap "Edit Message"
    3. Make your changes
    4. Tap "Save"
    5. Edited messages show "(edited)" label
- Delete Message:
    1. Long-press on your sent message
    2. Tap "Delete Message"
    3. Confirm deletion
- Reactions 
    1. long-press on the person's message and click an nemoji

Group Chats
Creating a Group:
1. Tap Chat → New
2. Select multiple contacts by tapping them
3. Selected contacts will have a checkmark
4. Enter a Group Name at the top
5. Tap "Create Group"

**Group Features:**
- See all members in the group
- Anyone can send messages
- Group admin can add/remove members
- Leave group anytime by tapping "Leave Group" in settings
